about=OSM Data Toolkit downloads OpenStreetMap data from the Overpass API for an area you draw on the map (or a bounding box) and turns it into ready-to-use QGIS layers, without leaving QGIS.
    Choose from categories such as points of interest, bus, rail and ferry stops, roads, railways, waterways, buildings, administrative boundaries, landuse and open public spaces. Each category is written as separate point, line and polygon layers.
    Features:
    - Draw a rectangle on the canvas or enter coordinates for the area of interest
    - Select the OSM feature categories to download
    - Export to GeoPackage (all layers in one file) or Shapefile
    - Optional local cache so repeat downloads are quick
    - Optional automatic loading of the results into the QGIS project
    - Works with large areas, with progress and a processing log
    
    
Notes: an internet connection is required. Very large areas can take a long time and may be limited by the public Overpass servers. Shapefiles cannot hold very many attribute columns, so GeoPackage is recommended for large areas. Data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).
